# Surface: Climate Detective web app (index.html, all screens)

Mode: Experience (a game; the data and the play lead from the first viewport).
Audience: Mexican teens 12–17; first judged by NASA Space Apps judges on laptops. Less formal than v1: a youthful game with attitude and a premium finish, never primary-school, never corporate, always readable.
Scope (confirmed Nov 2026): every screen gets the new type, animated buttons and motion; home is the showpiece.
Constraints: game text, data, cases and logic untouched; no framework, no build; self-hosted fonts only (OFL); reduced-motion path for everything.

## Shape brief (Nov 2026 evolution)

- Job: in one viewport a teen sees 45 years of their country's heat, plays with it, and wants to press JUGAR.
- Keep: real-data stripes, graphite ground, RdBu/BrBG colour meaning, zone triads, all copy.
- Change: type voices, button behaviour, page energy, celebration moments.
- Anti-goals: cartoon edu-game, neon hackathon, decoration that encodes nothing, motion that delays a task.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The warming stripes are the identity and now they talk back: the game's own name melts as the years warm. Refuses the space-navy "NASA hackathon" default and the cartoon edu-game; refuses the stiff museum-panel calm of v1.

OWN-WORLD: Deep graphite ground; every hue from RdBu (temperature) and BrBG (water). Three type voices, each with one job: Climate Crisis (variable YEAR axis, melts 1979→2050) for the game title only; Anybody (width 50–150, italic) black and wide-italic for headlines and buttons with sports-poster attitude, condensed for labels and numbers; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything a teen reads. Panels with layered soft shadows; slight resting tilt on case cards like files tossed on a desk.

STORY: The visitor watches the title melt while the cursor sweeps 1981→2025, scrubs the stripes and sees the letters freeze or melt with each year, realises the data is real and checkable, then plays a case.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed three-row stripes band (Veracruz, Pachuca, Monterrey) with scrub cursor and readouts; the giant Climate Crisis title sits on the band's lower edge, its YEAR axis bound to the scrubbed year; tagline; JUGAR (warm red, stripe-sweep hover) and Archive side by side; a slanted data ticker of each city's real Sen slope runs below.

FORM: Data-graphic identity (climate stripes), evolved in place: established world, approved system change of type, button grammar and motion; seed: none (pinned) — "Conservar y subir de nivel", "Juego juvenil con actitud", "Todas, con la portada como estrella".
Signature interaction: scrub the stripes and the title melts to that year. Motion grammar: stripe curtain between screens; every button has a hover (label roll, stripe sweep or shine) and a squash press; stripe-confetti when a medal is earned.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
