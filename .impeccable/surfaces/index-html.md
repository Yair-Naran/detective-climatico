# Surface: Climate Detective web app (index.html, all screens)

Mode: Experience (a game; the data and the play lead from the first viewport).
Audience: Mexican teens 12–17; first judged by NASA Space Apps judges on laptops. Must not feel childish, corporate, or hard to read.
Constraints: game text, data, cases and logic untouched; no framework, no build; self-hosted fonts only; emojis may become drawn SVG icons; logo/hero/scenes may be redrawn.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The warming stripes are the identity: real NASA POWER anomalies, not decoration. Refuses the space-navy-with-neon "NASA hackathon" default and the cartoon edu-game.

OWN-WORLD: Deep graphite-ink ground; colour comes only from diverging climate scales: RdBu (cold blues to warm reds) for temperature, BrBG (brown to teal) for rain/drought. Zones: Costa = deep blue, Centro = warm red, Norte = dry ochre-brown. Archivo (expanded black for display, condensed caps for labels, tabular figures for numbers) + Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading. Elevated graphite panels with layered shadows and a stripe edge; line icons drawn in code.

STORY: The visitor sees 45 years of their country's temperature as colour, scrubs it to read real values, understands that claims can be checked, then plays a case to act on it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed band of three stacked stripe rows (Veracruz, Pachuca, Monterrey, 1981–2025, anomaly vs 1991–2020) with city labels and live Sen-slope per decade; pointer/touch scrub shows a cursor line + year/value readout. Below: huge expanded "Detective climático", one-line tagline, primary JUGAR (warm red) and Archive secondary side by side.

FORM: Data-graphic identity (climate stripes), position 1 of own list (Impeccable's pick, chosen by the user over assigned candidate 4); seed:067b2c43469b2cd7 (degraded roll, no challengers).
Signature interaction: stripe scrub with live readout; selection ripple in the card's stripe colour; cards lift and tilt toward the pointer with deepening layered shadow; screens enter with a staggered stripe-wipe reveal.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
